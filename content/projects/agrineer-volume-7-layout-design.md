## Overview

This project focused on the editorial layout for Volume 7 of Agrineer. The goal was to improve how longer articles, images, and supporting content were organized so the publication felt clearer and more cohesive in print.

## Approach

- Structured text and imagery to create a more balanced page composition.
- Adjusted layout flow to improve readability across dense editorial content.
- Worked with the team to strengthen consistency across pages and sections.

## Output

The result was a more refined print-ready volume that improved readability while giving the publication a stronger overall visual structure.
