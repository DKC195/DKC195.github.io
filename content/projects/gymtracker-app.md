## Overview

GymTrackerApp is a gym companion for iPhone. It gives users personalized workout plans, tracks completed exercises and personal records, and includes an interval timer, 30-day challenges, and optional reminders for workouts, hydration, and sleep.

## Role

I co-built the app with a friend. I handled the backend on Firebase, and my friend built the iOS app.

## Outcomes

- Released on the App Store as a free app.
- Shipped a working consumer app end to end as a two-person team.
