## Overview

This project focused on the editorial layout for Volume 8 of Agrineer rather than interface design. The goal was to make long-form content easier to read, more visually coherent, and better suited for print.

## Approach

- Structured text and imagery to create a balanced page rhythm.
- Refined layouts to prioritize readability over decorative density.
- Worked with stakeholders to improve consistency across the publication.

## Output

The result was a print-ready volume that paired stronger editorial readability with a more polished visual presentation.
