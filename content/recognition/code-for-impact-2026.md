## About the program

Code for Impact is a six-month program for young technologists and early-stage founders in Nepal. It was implemented by Aadyanta Advisory with support from the U.S. Embassy Nepal, in partnership with AmCham Nepal and technology partners. Unlike a typical hackathon, it continues after demo day. Teams go through bootcamps, provincial hackathons across all seven provinces, and months of mentorship and field validation before a national showcase in Kathmandu.

## Role

I was the Lead Engineer for Neptou, a travel discovery app for Nepal. I led engineering across the backend and iOS app from the provincial hackathon through the national showcase.

## Result

- Neptou made it through each stage: 190 applicant teams, then 35 selected teams, 14 semi-finalists, and 7 national finalists.
- Named one of four **national winners** at the showcase in Kathmandu in July 2026.
- The win came with a US$1,000 seed grant, US$5,000 in AWS cloud credits, access to AI platforms, and continued business and technical mentoring.
